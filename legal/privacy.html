BuildKit Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

BuildKit ("the app") is a library of DIY build plans with resizable cut lists, shopping lists and animated step-by-step guides. This policy explains what data the app handles. In short: there is no account, no advertising and no tracking.

Data stored only on your device

  • Your settings (language, region, units), chosen sizes and finishes, step progress, saved ideas and boards.
  • Community content you create in this version (ratings, comments, "I built this" posts, photos you attach, your display name and blocked users). It is saved on your phone only and is not uploaded.

Deleting the app deletes this data.

Camera and photos

If you add a photo to a post, the app asks for camera or photo-library access. Photos stay on your device; we do not receive them.

Purchases

Build plans are one-time in-app purchases processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. To unlock plans and restore them on your devices, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history for this app and basic device information (such as country, app version and OS version). This is used only to provide and restore your purchases.

3D and AR models

To show a project in your room (AR), the app downloads the 3D model from our static file host (GitHub Pages). Like any web server, the host may log your IP address and request time.

What we do not do

  • No user accounts, no analytics SDKs, no advertising, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share personal data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

If a future version adds online accounts or shared community features, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
