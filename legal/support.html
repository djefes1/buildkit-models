Common questions

How do I get my purchased plans on a new phone?

Open Settings → Purchases → Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.

Is this a subscription?

No. Each plan is a one-time purchase that never renews.

Can I change the size after buying?

Yes. Change the size sliders at any time; the cut list, shopping list and steps recalculate.

AR doesn't open

AR needs an internet connection to download the model and an iPhone that supports AR Quick Look.
